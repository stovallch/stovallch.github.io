## Who are we?

**Stovall Clear and Haul** is a debris removal company, locally owned and operated in Montana.

We serve the Yellowstone and Carbon county areas with pride, as a local buisness we offer friendly and honest work. 

## What do we do?

We offer a range of debris and junk **clearing and hauling** services, including but not limited to:

- Property cleanup
- Junk removal
- Construction site clean up
- Demo cleanup
- Wildfire mitigation

And much more! If it is unwanted, we will pick it up and take it away. 

## How do we do it?

We have a one ton truck and an 8' x 14' dump trailer. We also have the ability to rent and operate most equipment.

## Why do we do it?

Family, comunity and faith.
